# LED strip calculator

- Drive calculator options and results from live catalogue products, not hardcoded SKUs or wattage lists.
- Classify strips, neon, and drivers from category/name/path (COB Strips, SMD Strips, Neon Flex, Flex Panel Sheets, Drivers) plus `product_features`. Match both `Color Temperature` and `Colour Temperature`, and both `Dimension` and `Dimensions`.
- Skip thumbnail-less fake WordPress images. Use each product's stored image URL and product code.
